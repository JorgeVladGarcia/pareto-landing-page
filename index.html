Who it's for

Built for founders who've become the bottleneck

This is for you if…

  • You run a profitable business doing $100K+ a year.
  • Most decisions, follow-ups and "quick" tasks still go through you.
  • Promises live in your memory, and some slip.
  • How things get done lives in your head, not in an SOP.

It's not for you if…

  • You're pre-revenue or still finding product-market fit.
  • You want a cheap, part-time task-taker.
